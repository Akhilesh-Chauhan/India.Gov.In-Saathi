## Findings for the developers

1. **Suggested links are unrelated to the question.** Most answers carry the same few buttons (112.gov.in, 8cpc.gov.in,
   aaccc.gov.in, agmarknet, depwd, eshram, gst, mea): they look like the first registry entries in alphabetical order.
   This drives the "no official next step" failures (G07, 259 cases) and most Response-flow failures (109 of 110).
2. **Outdated text is chosen over current text.** In 53 rows the judge found the facts wrong although every sentence is
   on the bot's own sources: the index holds both versions and the bot picks the old one (Tatkaal fee Rs 2,000 vs
   Rs 2,500; "18 instalments" for PM-Kisan vs the 23rd of 20 Jun 2026; ITR due date 31 Oct vs 21 Nov 2026; PVC Aadhaar
   Rs 50 vs Rs 75; PMFBY "compulsory for loanees"; NSAP Rs 200). Freshness and precedence (G04, G05) need a ranking signal.
   IDs: `eval/runs/indexed-v2-full-20261105/golden_review_candidates.json`.
3. **An unofficial blog is in the index** (`mylpg-in`, 15 WordPress pages including "PM Kisan 24th Installment Date 2026")
   and is cited for PM-Kisan dates (Q-153, Q-155, Q-422).
4. **Fake Central schemes:** the bot denies them but then invents State variants (Q-354 Bihar laptops, Q-355 a UP
   "Berojgari Bhatta").
5. **Language:** Hinglish questions are often answered in English or Devanagari (21 failures name it); Hindi and
   Hinglish facts-right rates are less than half of English.
6. **Conversations (0 of 13):** the bot answers at once instead of asking the one clarifying question the flow needs
   (MT-01, 06, 13, 14), asks an irrelevant one (MT-15 asks the social category), loses the safety-first order (MT-07),
   or answers turns without any source card (MT-04, MT-05).
7. **Latency** is above target: p50 8.0 s, p95 14.2 s.
8. **Guardrail templates** sometimes do not fit the question (OTP case Q-406 gets the "can't help break the law" text;
   Q-382 "submit my PF claim" gets a generic refusal without the UMANG link).
