QUESTION: What is the Unified Pension Scheme and who can opt for it?
EXPECTED ANSWER NOW:
Answer: The Unified Pension Scheme (UPS) is an option under NPS for Central Government employees. It was notified by the Ministry of Finance on 24.01.2025 (F. No. FX-1/3/2024-PR), took effect on 1 April 2025, is regulated by PFRDA, and its rules were notified 02.09.2025. Main features:
- Contributions: employee 10% of (Basic + DA); matching Government 10%; plus about 8.5% to a pool corpus.
- Assured payout: 50% of the average basic pay of the last 12 months, for 25 or more years of qualifying service; proportionate between 10 and 25 years.
- Minimum payout: Rs 10,000 a month after at least 10 years of service.
- Family payout: 60% to the legally wedded spouse.
- Dearness Relief on both the payout and the family payout.
- Lump sum: 1/10 of (last Basic + DA) for every completed 6 months of service, plus gratuity.
- Final withdrawal: up to 60% of the corpus, which reduces the payout proportionately.
Who can opt: (a) Central Government employees covered by NPS and in service on 01.04.2025; (b) new recruits joining on or after 01.04.2025, within 30 days of joining; (c) retired NPS subscribers who superannuated/retired on or before 31.03.2025 with at least 10 years of qualifying service or under FR 56(j) (not as a penalty), or their legally wedded spouse if the retiree has died. These past retirees get arrears with PPF-rate interest. The option deadline for existing employees and past retirees was 30 November 2025 (extended from 30.06.2025, then 30.09.2025); no further extension was found on official sources as of 15 Oct 2026. Anyone who does not opt stays in NPS. UPS carries a one-time, one-way switch back to NPS.

Steps (how to apply): 1. Apply to the State/UT department that implements NSAP, through the Gram Panchayat/Block (rural) or the Municipality (urban). Verification (about 15 days), then discussion in the Gram Sabha/Ward Sabha, then a sanction order by the Sanctioning Authority. The total time from receipt to sanction or rejection should not exceed 60 days, and appeal is possible if rejected. 4. Track the status on nsap.dord.gov.in (Track Application).
