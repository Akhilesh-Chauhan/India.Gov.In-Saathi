=== Q-046 | P2 | English | Scheme / fact detail (6.2) | expected site: eci.gov.in (Crawled)
GOLDEN-FACT STATUS: Verified on official page (15 Oct 2026); RE-VERIFY ON DEMO DAY
QUESTION: Can I vote while working abroad? Is there postal voting for NRIs?
EXPECTED ANSWER NOW:
Answer: Yes, you can be registered, but you must vote in person. An Indian citizen absent from India owing to employment, education etc., who has not acquired citizenship of another country and is otherwise eligible, can enrol as an Overseas (NRI) Elector at the address in their passport by filing Form 6A (online on voters.eci.gov.in with the required proofs, or on paper in 2 copies - free at Indian Missions - in person before the ERO/AERO or by post with self-attested copies). Documents: recent passport-size colour photo; self-attested copies of passport pages with photo, Indian address and other particulars; and the page with the valid visa endorsement. The BLO verifies at the passport address (or via the Indian Mission if no relative is available). An overseas elector is not issued an EPIC and can vote in person at the polling station on production of the original passport. There is currently no postal ballot for NRI voters: the ECI page only says e-postal ballots 'shall soon be provided'. One person can be on only one electoral roll - a registered general voter must surrender the EPIC with Form 6A, and can re-register as a general elector on returning to India.

Fail if: says NRIs can vote by post/online/at embassies; says Form 6 instead of 6A; says an EPIC is issued to overseas electors; omits the original passport requirement at the booth.
VERIFICATION NOTES: Trap: ECI page says 'e-postal ballots shall soon be provided to NRI Voters' - a statement of intent, not an available facility; the ETPBS page applies to service voters only. Recheck if ECI notifies NRI postal voting.
