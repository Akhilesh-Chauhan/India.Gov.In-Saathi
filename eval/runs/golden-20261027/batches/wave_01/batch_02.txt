QUESTION: How do I get an Import Export Code and what is the fee? Does it need annual updating?
EXPECTED ANSWER NOW:
Answer: The IEC is a 10-character code, the same as the firm's PAN, issued by DGFT on an online application (ANF-2A) at dgft.gov.in. It is needed for import/export of goods unless exempted. For services exports it is needed only when claiming FTP benefits. Pre-requisites: firm PAN, a bank account in the firm's name, and a valid address (DGFT may verify the address physically). The applicant needs an active DSC or Aadhaar (e-sign) of the proprietor/partner/director. Fee: Rs 500, paid online via Bharatkosh. The IEC is auto-generated and sent by email/SMS, and the e-IEC can be downloaded from the dashboard. Only one IEC per PAN. The IEC has permanent validity unless suspended or cancelled, but annual updating IS required: under FTP 2023 para 2.05(d)-(e), the IEC holder must update IEC details online every year during April-June, and must confirm online even if nothing has changed. An IEC not updated in time is de-activated and can be re-activated on successful updation. Any change in constitution, address, bank or other primary details must be updated within 30 days (HBP para 2.14).
Steps: 1. Go to dgft.gov.in > Services > IEC Profile Management > 'Apply for IEC' and register/log in. 2. Fill ANF-2A (firm details, PAN with name/DOB as per PAN, address, bank, branches, contact). 3. Upload a cancelled cheque/bank certificate and address proof. 4. Sign with DSC or Aadhaar e-Sign. 5. Pay the Rs 500 fee on Bharatkosh. 6. Download the e-IEC from the dashboard. 7. Every April-June: log in and use 'Update/Modify IEC' to update or confirm the details.
Behaviour: Cites DGFT FTP/HBP and the IEC manual. Does not ask for the user's PAN.
Fail if: Says the IEC is free or costs Rs 250/1,000; says no annual update is needed ('lifetime validity' without the April-June update rule); invents a renewal fee; recommends paid agents.
