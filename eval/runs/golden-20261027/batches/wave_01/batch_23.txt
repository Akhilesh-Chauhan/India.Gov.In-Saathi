=== Q-270 | P0 | English | Service procedure (6.2) | expected site: swavlambancard.gov.in (Crawled)
GOLDEN-FACT STATUS: Verified on official page (15 Oct 2026)
QUESTION: How do I apply for a UDID card and what disability percentage is needed?
EXPECTED ANSWER NOW:
Answer: Apply online on the UDID portal (swavlambancard.gov.in -> 'Apply for Disability Certificate & UDID Card'), or via the UDID app, camps or the CMO office. Aadhaar is mandatory for applying (notifications of 2023). Process: (1) submit the application on the portal; (2) when status shows 'Submitted', visit the UDID/disability certification section of the hospital chosen in the application; (3) the hospital/medical authority verifies the application; (4) specialist doctor(s) assess the disability; (5) the Medical Board reviews and fixes the type and percentage of disability; (6) the medical authority generates the disability certificate and UDID card, and DEPwD dispatches the card by Speed Post. The status can be tracked on the portal. Disability percentage: no minimum percentage is needed to get a disability certificate / UDID card. DEPwD (05-07-2024) told States not to deny certificates/UDID cards to persons assessed below 40%. However, 'benchmark disability', i.e. not less than 40% of a specified disability (RPwD Act, 2016), is needed for most benefits, such as reservation and schemes for PwBD.
Steps: 1. Open swavlambancard.gov.in and click 'apply now' (Apply for UDID). 2. Fill the form and submit. 3. When status = Submitted, visit the selected hospital/CMO UDID section. 4. Get the application verified. 5. Attend the specialist assessment. 6. The Medical Board decides type and percentage. 7. The card is generated and sent by Speed Post. Track via 'Track Your Application'.
