=== Q-012 | P1 | Hindi (Devanagari) | Current rule or circular (6.2) | expected site: uidai.gov.in (Crawled)

QUESTION: पैन को आधार से लिंक करने की आखिरी तारीख क्या है? न किया तो क्या होगा?
EXPECTED ANSWER NOW:
Answer: Under section 139AA of the Income-tax Act, every individual allotted PAN as on 1 July 2017 and eligible for Aadhaar had to link PAN with Aadhaar by 30 June 2023. That last date has passed; PANs not linked became inoperative from 1 July 2023 and remain inoperative until linked. Linking is still possible on the e-Filing portal (incometax.gov.in, Quick Links > "Link Aadhaar") after paying a fee of Rs 1,000 through e-Pay Tax (Assessment Year as applicable, Type of Payment "Other Receipts (500)"); if already paid, wait 4-5 working days before submitting the request. Consequences of an inoperative PAN (Circular No. 03 of 2023, 28 Mar 2023): no refund of tax due; no interest on such refund for the inoperative period; TDS and TCS deducted/collected at higher rates (sections 206AA/206CC). Exempt from linking: residents of Assam, Jammu & Kashmir and Meghalaya; non-residents under the Income-tax Act; persons aged 80 years or more at any time during the previous year; non-citizens (Notification 37/2017) - subject to later notifications. New PAN applicants are linked automatically at application.
Steps: 1. incometax.gov.in > Quick Links > Link Aadhaar; enter PAN and Aadhaar, Validate. 2. If "Payment details not found", click "Continue to Pay Through e-Pay Tax", pay Rs 1,000 (Other Receipts (500)). 3. Return after 4-5 working days, Link Aadhaar > enter details > OTP > submit. 4. Check via Quick Links > "Link Aadhaar Status".
Behaviour: Route to the Income Tax Department e-Filing portal (not UIDAI) with citation and as-on date; say there is no new last date published on these pages; no personal tax advice.
Fail if: gives a future deadline (e.g. 31 March 2026) or says linking is free; cites only UIDAI; says PAN is 'cancelled' (official term is inoperative); omits the Rs 1,000 fee.
