GOLDEN-FACT STATUS: Verified on official page (15 Oct 2026)
QUESTION: What is the maximum I can deposit in SCSS and can I open a joint account with my wife?
EXPECTED ANSWER NOW:
Answer: Maximum deposit is Rs 30 lakh across all your SCSS accounts taken together. Minimum is Rs 1,000, in multiples of Rs 1,000. Rs 30 lakh replaced Rs 15 lakh via G.S.R. 240(E) dated 31.03.2023. For retirees aged 55-60 the deposit is limited to the retirement benefits received or Rs 30 lakh, whichever is lower. Yes, a joint account can be opened, but only with the spouse. The first holder's age decides eligibility; there is no age limit for the second (spouse) holder. The whole deposit in a joint account is attributed to the first holder, so it counts against the first holder's Rs 30 lakh limit. If both spouses are individually eligible, each can open own single/joint accounts up to Rs 30 lakh each. Eligibility: 60+; or 55-60 retired on superannuation/VRS (open within 1 month of receiving benefits); or 50+ for retired Defence personnel (excluding civilian Defence employees).
Behaviour: Limit and joint rule each cited to NSI/SCSS Rules 2019. No eligibility verdict for the user.
Fail if: says Rs 15 lakh; says a joint account can be with a son or daughter; says the limit is Rs 60 lakh per couple in one joint account.

=== Q-289 | P2 | English | Scheme / fact detail (6.2) | expected site: online-inspire.gov.in (Crawled)
GOLDEN-FACT STATUS: Verified on official page (15 Oct 2026)
QUESTION: Who is eligible for the INSPIRE SHE scholarship and how much is it?
EXPECTED ANSWER NOW:
Answer: INSPIRE Scholarship for Higher Education (SHE), Department of Science & Technology: 12,000 scholarships a year (from the SHE-2017 batch onwards). Each is worth Rs 80,000 per year: Rs 5,000 per month (Rs 60,000 per year) plus a Rs 20,000 per year summer-research mentorship grant. Support lasts up to 5 years from the 1st year of B.Sc./B.S./B.Sc. with Research (4 years)/Int. M.Sc./Int. M.S., or until the course is completed, whichever is earlier. Continuation depends on academic performance. Eligible (any one): (a) aggregate marks within the top 1% of the Class XII Board (State/Central) and pursuing Basic/Natural Science at B.Sc., B.S., B.Sc. (Research, 4-yr) or Int. M.Sc./M.S. level; (b) JEE (IIT) rank holders or AIPMT top-10,000 rank holders pursuing such courses; (c) top 1% in Class XII and doing Int. M.S. at IISERs, NISER, UM-DAE CEBS Mumbai or Visva-Bharati; (d) KVPY scholars; (e) NTSE, JBNSTS scholars and International Olympiad medallists pursuing natural science. Only listed subjects qualify (Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, Biology, Statistics, Geology, Astrophysics, Astronomy, Electronics, Botany, Zoology, Biochemistry, Anthropology, Microbiology, Geophysics, Geochemistry, Atmospheric and Oceanic Sciences). Engineering, Medicine, Biotechnology, Computer Science, distance mode and similar courses are not supported. The student must apply in the same year they pass Class XII and enrol. Apply online on online-inspire.gov.in. Payment goes by DBT to an SBI account in the scholar's own name.
