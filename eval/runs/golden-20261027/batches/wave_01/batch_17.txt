QUESTION: What are the verticals under PMAY-Urban 2.0?
EXPECTED ANSWER NOW:
Answer: PMAY-Urban 2.0 has four verticals. (1) Beneficiary Led Construction (BLC): assistance to individual EWS families to build a new house of 30-45 sq m on their own land; States may give land patta to the landless; instalments are linked to geo-tagged construction stages. (2) Affordable Housing in Partnership (AHP): EWS families buy/get allotted houses in projects by public/private agencies; includes Redeemable Housing Vouchers for whitelisted private projects and in-situ slum redevelopment. (3) Affordable Rental Housing (ARH): Model-1 converts existing government-funded vacant houses into rental housing; Model-2 means construct, operate and maintain rental housing by private/public entities for urban poor, working women, industrial workers etc. (4) Interest Subsidy Scheme (ISS): home-loan interest subsidy for EWS/LIG/MIG, up to Rs 1.80 lakh. For BLC and AHP, central assistance is Rs 2.50 lakh/unit in NE/hill States (Rs 2.25 lakh Centre + at least Rs 0.25 lakh State) and in UTs with legislature (J&K, Puducherry, Delhi). Other UTs get Rs 2.50 lakh Centre. Other States get Rs 1.5 lakh Centre + at least Rs 1.0 lakh State. ARH gets a Technology Innovation Grant of Rs 3,000/sq m (Centre) + Rs 2,000/sq m (State). Guidelines dated September 2024.
