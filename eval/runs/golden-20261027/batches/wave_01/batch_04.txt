Answer: Yes. The Employment Linked Incentive (ELI) scheme, approved by the Cabinet on 1 July 2025, runs as the Pradhan Mantri Viksit Bharat Rozgar Yojana (PM-VBRY). It took effect on 1 August 2025 and was launched by the PM on 15 August 2025. Jobs created in the registration period 01.08.2025 to 31.07.2027 are covered. The outlay is Rs 99,446 crore, with a target of 3.5 crore+ jobs, and EPFO implements it. Part B (employers): for each additional employee above the establishment's baseline, with gross salary up to Rs 1 lakh at joining, the employer gets a monthly incentive for 2 years: EPF wage up to Rs 10,000: up to Rs 1,000 (10% of EPF wage below Rs 10,000); above Rs 10,000 to Rs 20,000: Rs 2,000; above Rs 20,000 to Rs 1 lakh: Rs 3,000. The manufacturing sector also gets years 3 and 4. Conditions: at least 2 additional employees if the baseline is below 50, or at least 5 if the baseline is 50 or more; each new hire (first-timer or re-joinee) must stay at least 6 months; payment is six-monthly in a lump sum after six ECRs are filed; no incentive for months in which the threshold is not met, and the period is not extended. Part A (employees): first-time employees earning up to Rs 1 lakh get one month's EPF wage, capped at Rs 15,000, in two instalments (after 6 and 12 months, the second after a financial-literacy course); part is kept in a savings instrument. New UANs must be generated through UMANG face authentication (from 01.08.2025). As on PIB 01 Aug 2026, the scheme completed one year with 72 lakh+ first-time employees.
Behaviour: gives scheme facts with dates and citations; no verdict on a particular employer's eligibility; directs registration to official EPFO/PM-VBRY channels and warns against fake portals (MoLE caution, 03 Sep 2025).
Fail if: says ELI is only approved or not yet launched; gives the original 2024 Budget ELI Scheme A/B/C structure as current; misstates the slabs or the 2-year/4-year periods; or cites an unofficial portal.
VERIFICATION NOTES: The scheme name changed: Cabinet approved it as 'Employment Linked Incentive (ELI) Scheme' on 1 July 2025; it is implemented as PM-VBRY (EPFO also uses 'Rozgar'/'Rojgar' spellings).
