Enter the pension details (PPO number, bank account, disbursing agency) and do a face scan. 4. An SMS with the Jeevan Pramaan ID confirms success; the bank fetches the DLC online. EXPECTED ANSWER NOW:
Answer: Central Government civil pensioners and family pensioners get additional pension/family pension by age slab, as a percentage of basic pension/family pension (O.M. No. 38/37/08-P&PW(A) dated 2.9.2008, effective 1.1.2006):

- 85 to less than 90: 30%
