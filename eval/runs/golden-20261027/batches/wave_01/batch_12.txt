QUESTION: How do I register for apprenticeship after BTech and how much stipend is paid?
EXPECTED ANSWER NOW:
Answer: BTech pass-outs register as a 'General student' on the NATS 2.0 portal (nats.education.gov.in, Ministry of Education/BOAT). Students still studying use the Sandwich/AEDP route. Training is 6 months to 1 year of on-the-job training with an establishment, followed by a Certificate of Proficiency from the Government of India. There is no guarantee of a job. The candidate should ideally have no previous work experience. Stipend: the minimum monthly stipend for Graduate/Degree apprentices is Rs 12,300 (revised from Rs 9,000 under GSR 610(E) of Sept 2025). BOAT notices state the revised rate applies under NATS w.e.f. 01.04.2026. Technician (diploma) apprentices get Rs 10,900 and Technician (vocational) Rs 9,600. These are minimums paid by the establishment. 50% of the stipend is reimbursed by the Government via DBT, which needs e-KYC and an Aadhaar-seeded bank account mapped in NPCI. Individual establishments may pay more (e.g. a BOAT(SR) notification offers Rs 10,000/12,300-type rates for its own seats).
Steps: 1. Open nats.education.gov.in and click 'Student' (top right). 2. Choose the 'General student' tab (for Diploma/Graduate pass-outs) and click 'Student Registration'. 3. Keep ready: personal email, mobile number, Aadhaar (for e-KYC), degree/provisional certificate PDF under 1 MB, and APAAR ID (create one on the ABC portal if needed). 4. Verify mobile and email by OTP. 5. Complete Aadhaar e-KYC with OTP on the Aadhaar-linked mobile. 6. Fill the registration form (institute, course, specialisation), set a password and submit. 7. Click the verification link sent by email to activate the account. 8. Log in and complete the profile: basic details, educational details (fetch the certificate from DigiLocker using APAAR or upload it), communication details, training preferences, then preview and final submit. 9. Apply to establishment advertisements, accept the contract within 15 days, and wait for BOAT approval.
Behaviour: steps and stipend from NATS pages and BOAT notices with a citation. Gives no promise of a placement.
