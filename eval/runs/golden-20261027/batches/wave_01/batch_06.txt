QUESTION: Within how many days must I e-verify my return after filing?
EXPECTED ANSWER NOW:
Answer: Within 30 days of uploading the return (Notification No. 2/2024 dated 31.03.2024; ITD FAQ). If verified within 30 days, the upload date counts as the filing date. If verified after 30 days, the date of e-verification / receipt of ITR-V at CPC is treated as the filing date, and all late-filing consequences follow (e.g., late fee). A return never verified is invalid, although a condonation request can be made for genuine delay. Example: uploaded 5 July 2025 and verified 10 August 2025, so the filing date is 10 August 2025. Under the 2025 Act (s.265) there is no substantive change in the manner of verification.
Behaviour: Cite the ITD FAQ. No personal determination.
Fail if: says 120 days (the pre-August 2022 rule); says late verification has no consequence.
VERIFICATION NOTES: Other ITD pages cite 'w.e.f. 01/08/2022'; the FAQ cites Notification 2/2024. Both give 30 days.
