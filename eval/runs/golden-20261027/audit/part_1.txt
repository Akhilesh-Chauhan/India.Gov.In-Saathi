=== Q-065 | Current rule or circular (6.2) | Can I still file an updated return for AY 2022-23, and what additional tax applies?
EXPECTED ANSWER BEFORE: Answer: Yes, subject to conditions. Additional tax (s.140B) is a % of aggregate tax + interest: 25% within 12 months; 50% within 12-24 months; 60% within 24-36 months; 70% within 36-48 months from the end of the relevant AY. ITR-U is not allowed if it: results in a loss, lower tax liability or (higher) refund; follows search/survey/requisition; covers a year where assessment or reassessment is pending or completed; follows prosecution; or follows a s.148A show-cause notice issued after 36 months (unless the order says it is not a fit case).
