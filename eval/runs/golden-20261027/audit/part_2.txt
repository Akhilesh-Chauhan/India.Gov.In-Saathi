=== Q-051 | Current rule or circular (6.2) | Which law governs income earned in FY 2026-27 - the Income-tax Act 1961 or the Income-tax Act 2025 - and what is a 'tax year'?
EXPECTED ANSWER BEFORE: Answer: Income earned in FY 2026-27 is governed by the Income-tax Act, 2025, which came into force on 01.04.2026. The 1961 Act stands repealed from that date. Its provisions continue under the savings clause, s.536, for tax years beginning before 1 April 2026 (e.g., AY 2026-27 returns, pending assessments). A 'tax year' is the twelve-month financial year starting 1 April. It is shorter for a business or income source started during the year: it runs from that start date to 31 March. 'Tax year' replaces 'previous year'. The 2025 Act has no 'assessment year'. Tax Year 2026-27 = FY 2026-27. Per ITD FAQ 'Are AY 2026-27 and Tax Year 2026-27 the same?': No, these are two separate obligations. AY 2026-27 covers income of FY 2025-26, filed under the 1961 Act on old ITR forms; its due date was 31 July 2026 (31 Aug for certain non-audit cases). Tax Year 2026-27 covers income of FY 2026-27 under the 2025 Act; that return is not due until July 2027. Advance tax for TY 2026-27 (from June 2026) is under the 2025 Act. Both returns must be filed; filing one does not cover the other.

EXPECTED ANSWER BEFORE: Answer: Under the Modified Interest Subvention Scheme (MISS), short-term crop loans and loans for allied activities (animal husbandry, dairy, fisheries, bee-keeping) through a KCC are given at a concessional 7% per annum, up to an overall limit of Rs 3 lakh. The Government pays lending institutions 1.5% interest subvention. Farmers who repay on time get an extra 3% per annum Prompt Repayment Incentive (PRI), so the effective rate is 4% per annum. Timely means within the due date, subject to a maximum of 1 year from disbursement; farmers who repay after one year do not get the PRI. Farmers engaged only in allied activities have a sub-limit of Rs 2 lakh, and the crop-loan component takes priority. This is per the RBI MISS circular for FY 2025-26 (13 Jan 2026), the latest MISS document on agriwelfare.gov.in. Interest above Rs 3 lakh is at the bank's normal rate. Collateral-free KCC loans: up to Rs 2 lakh from 1 Jan 2025.
